# Method lineage

PocketMBA is a composition and extension of established mechanisms, not a claim that business strategy lacked prior art.

## Imported mechanisms

- **Jobs to Be Done:** Recover the progress or job organizing behavior instead of beginning from demographics or solution categories. Christensen Institute: <https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/jobs-to-be-done/>
- **Value Proposition Canvas and Business Model Canvas:** Decompose hypotheses about customers, value, resources, channels, and economics so they can be tested. Strategyzer: <https://www.strategyzer.com/value-proposition>
- **Nadler-Tushman congruence and McKinsey 7-S:** Treat performance as fit among interdependent organizational elements, not the excellence of isolated parts. McKinsey: <https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/people-and-organizational-performance/our-insights/a-new-operating-model-for-a-new-world>
- **Porter on strategy:** Look for tradeoffs, deliberate absence, and reinforcing fit rather than operational-effectiveness parity. Harvard Business Review: <https://hbr.org/1996/11/what-is-strategy>
- **Rumelt's strategy kernel:** Separate diagnosis, guiding policy, and coherent action. Profile Books: <https://profilebooks.com/work/good-strategy-bad-strategy/>
- **Blue Ocean strategy canvas:** Compare the shape of value delivery across factors rather than merely enumerating competitors. Blue Ocean Strategy: <https://www.blueoceanstrategy.com/tools/strategy-canvas/>
- **Wardley Mapping:** Make dependencies, user need, and component evolution visible; treat the landscape as moving. Wardley Maps: <https://www.swardleymaps.com/>
- **Product-market-fit and continuous discovery practice:** Use behavior and repeated learning to test strategic hypotheses. Superhuman: <https://blog.superhuman.com/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit/>; Product Talk: <https://www.producttalk.org/discovering-solutions/>
- **Business architecture:** Connect strategy to capabilities and operating model. Business Architecture Guild: <https://www.businessarchitectureguild.org/page/whitepapers>

## The surviving contribution

Existing tools largely provide canvases, artifact generators, stage-specific workflows, or individual analyses. PocketMBA's contribution is the adaptive control layer and the explained-residual differential:

1. derive the reference population from the undertaking's actual job and choice set;
2. compare whole-system relations across product, market, identity, distribution, economics, capability, and governance;
3. causally distinguish strategic omission, differentiation, moat, debt, drift, and compensatory prosthesis;
4. retain market motion and adversarial response;
5. scale from a delegated check to assured analysis without making the user choose a workflow or accept a composite score.

This novelty claim is bounded. It concerns the integrated agent behavior, not invention of its constituent strategy methods.
