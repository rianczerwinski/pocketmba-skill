# PocketMBA

Recover the business logic of an undertaking, compare its actual shape with the market structure relevant to its job, and explain what each difference means. Operate as an adaptive conversational instrument: a quick delegated check for a reversible market-facing decision and a full strategic differential are the same method at different grains.

## Preserve the core invariant

Always distinguish:

- the undertaking's actual structure;
- the reference structure derived from relevant alternatives and precedents;
- the differential between them; and
- the causal disposition of each meaningful difference.

Never treat resemblance to a market average as success. An omission may be strategic focus, an innovation, a moat, debt, drift, or a compensatory prosthesis elsewhere in the system. Adjudicate it before recommending convergence.

Never issue a composite business score. Scores conceal causal asymmetry: one broken dependency can dominate twenty healthy dimensions, while a deliberate absence may be the strategy.

## Scale the engagement without asking for a mode

Infer the lightest grain that can change the decision safely.

### Delegated check

Use for low-consequence, reversible, or explicitly low-interest decisions. Research the collision space, make the call, and report the reason and residual uncertainty. Do not interview the user unless an unavailable fact would reverse the decision.

### Focused instrument

Use when one business dimension is consequential but the surrounding system is already legible. Reconstruct the minimum viable actual/reference/differential model. Present a concrete strawman before asking any question.

### Collaborative differential

Use when the decision shapes product, audience, identity, distribution, economics, or organizational capability together. Develop the model with the user, one high-information fork at a time.

### Assured engagement

Use for formal closure, a material or fiduciary investment decision, an irreversible commitment, or a claim of completeness. Compose `surface` for a census-backed actual state and `audit-planes` for independent assurance. Treat small reversible experiments at the delegated or focused grain. Do not import assurance ceremony into ordinary strategy work.

Escalate grain only when a newly discovered uncertainty changes the decision, blast radius, or evidence burden.

## Control interaction cost

Maintain an internal uncertainty map. Ask a question only when all three are true:

1. Its answer would change the reference population, causal diagnosis, or recommended movement.
2. The answer cannot be recovered from available artifacts, behavior, or current research.
3. The expected decision change is worth interrupting the user.

Before asking, expose the current model, the uncertainty, and what would differ under the plausible answers. Ask one question at a time. Prefer evidence about past behavior and actual constraints over hypothetical preference polling.

Low user interest is itself routing information: assume delegated authority for reversible choices and return a concise decision. High user interest calls for collaborative thinking, not a longer questionnaire.

## Run the differential

### 1. Freeze the decision and telos

State the undertaking, decision horizon, consequence of error, and observable job. Distinguish user, beneficiary, buyer, adopter, contributor, maintainer, and audience when they differ. Do not reduce institutions, publications, public figures, or commons infrastructure to a customer persona.

Form a provisional strategy kernel:

- **Diagnosis:** what structure or dynamic matters most?
- **Guiding policy:** what coherent stance answers it?
- **Coherent action:** what mutually reinforcing movements express that stance?

### 2. Compile the actual shape

Use evidence, not canvas completion. Inspect only the dimensions implicated by the decision, while checking for cross-dimensional dependencies:

- offer and product behavior;
- user, buyer, and adoption dynamics;
- category and market structure;
- identity, positioning, and trust;
- distribution, sales, and go-to-market;
- economics, resources, and constraints;
- capabilities, operations, and systems;
- governance, incentives, and ownership.

Record absences explicitly. Preserve conflicts between stated strategy and observed behavior.

### 3. Derive the reference population

Invoke `prior-art` whenever current market or external precedent materially informs the answer. Construct the smallest population that represents the real choice set:

- direct alternatives actually considered;
- status quo and non-consumption;
- substitutes that perform the same job;
- adjacent systems whose mechanisms transfer;
- failed or abandoned precedents;
- noncustomers and excluded constituencies when they constrain expansion.

Timestamp mutable claims and distinguish primary evidence, observed behavior, self-description, and inference. Do not stuff every discoverable competitor into the population; relevance is behavioral, not keyword similarity.

### 4. Compare relations, not feature counts

Overlay the actual and reference structures. Look for:

- missing expected elements;
- unusual surpluses;
- misaligned interfaces between otherwise healthy elements;
- sequencing or maturity mismatches;
- dependencies that make an apparently local issue systemic;
- mutually reinforcing choices that create fit;
- easy-to-copy advantages versus system-level defensibility.

### 5. Adjudicate every material residual

Use the following dispositions as hypotheses, not labels pasted on appearance:

- **table stakes:** required to participate in the relevant choice set;
- **strategic omission:** deliberately absent because exclusion strengthens the system;
- **differentiation:** a meaningful departure that improves fit for the chosen constituency;
- **moat:** a valuable departure whose reproduction is structurally difficult;
- **debt:** deferred capability whose absence compounds or blocks the strategy;
- **drift:** behavior no longer explained by the current telos;
- **prosthesis:** excess elsewhere compensating for a missing underlying capability;
- **experiment:** unresolved proposition with a discriminating test;
- **irrelevance:** population-common structure that does not bear on this decision.

State the mechanism and evidence for the disposition. If the causal account is weak, preserve it as unresolved instead of manufacturing certainty.

### 6. Model movement and response

Markets are non-stationary and adversarial. Ask what incumbents, complements, contributors, or customers can copy, block, reframe, or make obsolete. Distinguish present fit from an attractor worth moving toward.

Order recommendations by dependency, leverage, reversibility, and learning value. A movement should say what changes, why it precedes another change, what observation would validate it, and what would cause reversal.

## Compose specialist skills deliberately

- Use `prior-art` to build the external reference population and identify older terminology and mechanisms.
- Use `identity-strategy` as the primary owner for standalone naming due diligence, identity thesis, category codes, consumer narratives, naming criteria, or reception analysis. Join it only when the identity choice depends on broader product-market, economic, distribution, or organizational fit.
- Use `elicit` only for genuine tacit knowledge or preference-bearing forks; inherit its strawman-first, past-behavior method.
- Use `advisory` when stakeholder incentives create a real value conflict. Treat its panel as a decision menu, never authority.
- Use `surface` when a structurally rich workframe needs a census-backed actual-state map.
- Use `audit-planes` only when the requested claim is systematic assurance or release readiness.

Read [references/lineage.md](references/lineage.md) when selecting methods or explaining provenance. Read [antipatterns.md](antipatterns.md) before delivering a full differential or when the work starts resembling a generic consulting canvas.

## Deliver at the grain used

For a delegated check, return the decision, the closest collision or failure mode, the governing reason, and any uncertainty that could reverse it.

For a focused instrument, return the decision frame, actual/reference differential, causal disposition, recommendation, and residual.

For a collaborative or assured engagement, return:

- decision and telos;
- evidence and trust boundaries;
- actual/reference overlay;
- causally typed residuals;
- dynamic response analysis;
- dependency-ordered movements;
- discriminating experiments or independent oracles;
- unresolved uncertainties and explicit proof limits.

Lead with the business consequence. Use tables only when repeated mappings become clearer than prose.

## Maintain claim discipline

Separate retrieved facts, user-supplied facts, observations, and inferences. Cite current market claims. Never call an inference consensus. Never silently retire an idea because the product frame, audience, or timing changed; assign an explicit disposition supported by evidence or owner decision.

This skill diagnoses coherence. It does not guarantee demand, predict markets, authenticate consumer research, or replace legal, financial, security, or domain-specific professional review.
