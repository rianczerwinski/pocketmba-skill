# Antipatterns

### Canvas completion

❌ Wrong: Fill every familiar business box because the framework contains it.

✅ Right: Begin from the decision and job; add a dimension only when it changes the reference population, diagnosis, or movement.

Why it flips: Coverage is not relevance, and catalogs obscure causal dependencies.

### Empty interview

❌ Wrong: Ask the user to choose the depth, framework, audience, and desired artifact before presenting a model.

✅ Right: Infer grain, produce a falsifiable strawman, and ask only the highest-information unresolved fork.

Why it flips: The corrected form performs the analytical labor and reserves interruption for information that can change the answer.

### Competitor scrapbook

❌ Wrong: Treat every similar search result as part of the competitive set.

✅ Right: Include an alternative only with a mechanism explaining why it belongs in the behavioral choice population, including status quo and substitutes.

Why it flips: Keyword similarity does not establish that a user, buyer, contributor, or institution actually chooses between the alternatives.

### Average as ideal

❌ Wrong: Recommend every population-common feature or practice.

✅ Right: Causally adjudicate each omission and preserve deliberate absence and generative constraint.

Why it flips: Market norms include baggage, historical accidents, and choices incompatible with the undertaking's telos.

### Weighted maturity score

❌ Wrong: Compress the system into one score or traffic light.

✅ Right: Report typed residuals, dependency structure, and release-blocking conditions separately.

Why it flips: Healthy minor dimensions cannot compensate for a failed critical dependency, and strategic asymmetry is not deficiency.

### Artifact factory

❌ Wrong: Produce personas, PRDs, SWOTs, positioning statements, or canvases because business work is expected to emit them.

✅ Right: Emit only the representation required to change the decision or coordinate the next movement.

Why it flips: Otherwise the artifact becomes the goal and fossilizes an untested diagnosis.

### Metric monarchy

❌ Wrong: Let revenue, retention, survey score, growth, or contribution count stand for total coherence.

✅ Right: Use metrics as discriminating evidence for named hypotheses and preserve countervailing dimensions.

Why it flips: Metrics are observations inside a causal system and can be gamed, delayed, or locally optimized.

### Strategy by imitation

❌ Wrong: Import a successful precedent's visible practice without its constraints, dependencies, or stage.

✅ Right: Compare mechanisms and enabling structure, then classify the transfer as required, optional, incompatible, or experimental.

Why it flips: The copied element may be a consequence rather than a cause, or a prosthesis for conditions absent here.

### Scope-change erasure

❌ Wrong: Treat a change in audience, commercial posture, or timing as rejection of every capability formerly discussed.

✅ Right: Preserve each constituent as active, implemented, captured, deferred, superseded, or explicitly rejected, with evidence for terminal dispositions.

Why it flips: Reframing changes the present system boundary; it does not itself supply a disposition for every constituent.
